# XRD Phase Finder 1.3.2

## Multi-pattern publication figures

- Automatically render tall Multi-XRD exports so individual legends remain readable at the configured font size.
- Align per-pattern legends automatically or choose Right, Center or Left alignment in View settings.
- Drag individual legend boxes directly on the plot for final manual adjustment.
- Optionally hide phase names from per-pattern legends while keeping phase markers visible.
- Avoid strong peaks and neighbouring XRD profiles when selecting automatic legend positions.
- Preserve clear colored frames and consistent sample/phase colors in screen and exported figures.
- Prevent a macOS graphics crash when an observed pattern is selected directly on the plot.
